How it works

From link to protected access in three private steps

Your camera feed and authenticator secrets never leave the page. Only local descriptors and rotating codes are kept on this device to gate your link.

  1. 01Add accessEnrol trusted faces in the browser or create a rotating authenticator code.
  2. 02Store locallyFace descriptors and TOTP secrets stay in this browser — no photo or code is uploaded.
  3. 03Gate your linkShare a link that checks locally: faces are matched or codes are verified on this device.
Your data, your control

This demo has no application backend. Face descriptors and authenticator secrets are isolated to the browser and device where they were created — perfect for gating any link privately.
